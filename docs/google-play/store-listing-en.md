## Full description

ProxySmith helps you evaluate supported proxy configurations from a subscription and identify reliable, low-latency results.

Choose a built-in source, add a source you trust, or enter a subscription URL for a one-time run. ProxySmith downloads the subscription, validates and deduplicates supported links, samples the requested number of candidates, and tests them through the bundled Xray engine.

Candidates are measured over three progressively stronger rounds. Final ranking considers successful test ratio, median latency, and jitter rather than simply choosing whichever test finishes first.

Features:

- Support for VLESS, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, and Hysteria2 links supported by the bundled engine
- Reliability, median latency, jitter, and attempt counts
- Adjustable test count, latency limit, concurrency, and test endpoint
- Custom subscription-source management
- Copy, multi-select, and QR-code tools for results
- English and Persian interfaces with light and dark themes
- On-device processing with no account, advertising, analytics, or result-history collection

ProxySmith is a testing and ranking utility. Availability and behavior of third-party subscriptions, proxies, and test endpoints are outside the developer's control. Review their terms and security practices before use.
